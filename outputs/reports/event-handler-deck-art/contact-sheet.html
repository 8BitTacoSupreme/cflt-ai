Three corrections carried in the art

  1. ART-3 — the old tree could route an external sink to Transactional EOS via state? No → duplicate visible? Yes. EOS does not cross the cluster boundary, so that branch recommended something that cannot work. An “Is the effect inside Kafka?” node now gates both EOS terminals.
  2. ART-6 — the old ladder flowed retry topic → delay consumer → retry succeeded?, implying delay consumers run business logic. They wait and re-publish. Teams copy these diagrams, so the wiring matters.
  3. ART-11 — compatibility checking and Data Contract rules are now two distinct gates. That is what makes the “syntactic, not semantic” caveat on S25 visible rather than a verbal footnote.
